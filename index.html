Our Story & Mission

Born Out of Passion for Tinsukia Cricket

Cricket Turf on Makum Road was established by passionate local cricketers who grew up playing on bumpy open grounds across Upper Assam. During prolonged monsoon spells, cricket would come to a complete standstill for months.

We decided to build Tinsukia’s first true international-standard sports facility. By pairing 40mm imported monofilament astroturf with high-drainage sub-base aggregates and anti-glare stadium floodlights, we ensure your squad can play 365 days a year—rain or shine, day or night.
